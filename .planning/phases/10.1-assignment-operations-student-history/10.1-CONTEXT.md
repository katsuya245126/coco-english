# Phase 10.1: Assignment Operations & Student History - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-07-12
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 10.1 turns the existing teacher dashboard into an in-app assignment-operations workspace and makes completed mission history useful to students. Teachers get a combined cross-class review inbox, per-class review policy, unread/viewed/reviewed handling, an incomplete-work queue, and class/assignment drill-downs. Students get bounded Current/Past mission lists and a read-only recap of their own completed work.

This phase does not add email, push, or SMS notifications; change the speaking mission flow; introduce bulk grading; add student accounts; or replace the server-owned assignment status/audit model. The approved visual direction is part of this phase, while the later Phase 12 overhaul still owns the broader app-wide visual unification across voice, mascot, and Coco Chat.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Teacher Information Architecture and Visual Contract
- **D-01:** The default teacher home is a combined, cross-class **Needs review** inbox. It is the primary work surface, not a passive class-list dashboard.
- **D-02:** The approved desktop structure is a slim left sidebar plus one full-width content workspace. The sidebar contains Needs review, Incomplete, All activity, class shortcuts/counts, Create class, and Missions. Class pages use a focused version of the same queue model rather than a different interaction pattern.
- **D-03:** The implementation should match the approved web previews: Gmail-like rows rather than small dashboard cards, clear table hierarchy, blue unread treatment, status pills, class labels, received times, filters, and responsive collapse of the sidebar on small screens. Preserve this hierarchy and appearance; accessibility or real-data adjustments must not turn it back into competing dashboard panels.
- **D-04:** Needs review is newest-submission-first. Opening a submission clears its unread styling; viewing alone does not remove it from the queue. Explicit teacher review removes it.
- **D-05:** Review policy is configurable per class. **Review every submission** is the default. Teachers may switch a class to **review flagged submissions only**, in which case ordinary completions appear in activity history but do not remain in Needs review.
- **D-06:** Notifications are in-app only for this release. No email digest, immediate email, browser push, SMS, or external notification delivery.

### Review Processing
- **D-07:** An ordinary completed submission exposes two primary actions: **Mark reviewed** and **Request retry**. Do not add a third follow-up state.
- **D-08:** After Mark reviewed, return the teacher to the inbox. The reviewed row disappears and the count updates; do not auto-open the next submission and do not leave the teacher on the detail page.
- **D-09:** Do not provide bulk review in this release. A reviewed receipt means the teacher opened that student's evidence and explicitly reviewed it.
- **D-10:** Provide both recovery paths for accidental review: a short-lived Undo message immediately after Mark reviewed, plus a permanent **Move back to Needs review** action from All activity.
- **D-11:** The permanent restore action belongs in the reviewed submission's overflow dropdown, not as a persistent bottom-page button. Restoring the review task must not change the student's completed homework status.

### Inbox Freshness and In-App Notices
- **D-12:** Teacher queues and counts should detect new submissions automatically within about 30 seconds; true instantaneous delivery is not required.
- **D-13:** If new rows arrive while the inbox is visible, show a **new submissions** banner. Prepend/reveal those rows only when the teacher clicks the banner so the list does not shift while being read.
- **D-14:** Needs review counts stay fresh across all teacher pages, including class, assignment, and evidence views—not only on teacher home.
- **D-15:** When a submission arrives while the teacher is elsewhere in the teacher area, show a small non-blocking notice containing student, assignment, and class, with a View link. This complements the sidebar count and does not interrupt the current task.

### Incomplete Work
- **D-16:** Incomplete work has its own teacher-home queue and never mixes into Needs review. Group rows by assignment, with **Missed** first, **Due soon** second, and **Later** collapsed.
- **D-17:** Due soon means due within 24 hours. Assignments with no due date belong in collapsed Later.
- **D-18:** Within an assignment, each unfinished student shows a distinct **Not started** or **Started** status pill.
- **D-19:** The sidebar Incomplete count is the number of outstanding student-assignment items. One student owing two assignments contributes two items.
- **D-20:** Clicking an incomplete student row opens that assignment's results with the selected student highlighted, preserving assignment context and nearby classmates.

### Student Current and Past Missions
- **D-21:** Student home has two tabs: **Current** and **Past missions**. Use pages rather than an indefinitely growing list.
- **D-22:** Only completed assignments move to Past. Late and missed-but-still-open work remains Current because late submission remains allowed. Canceled assignments stay hidden.
- **D-23:** Current ordering is: Retry first, then due soon, then later/no due date. Teacher-review work remains Current with a waiting/review state; it is not treated as completed history.
- **D-24:** Past is ordered by completion time, newest first, with five missions per page.
- **D-25:** A Past row opens a read-only **View what I said** recap scoped to the currently unlocked student. The recap reconstructs the final completed conversation using Coco's prompts, student transcripts, replayable recordings, and available pronunciation feedback.
- **D-26:** If the student completed only after a retry, the child-facing recap shows the final completed attempt only. Earlier attempts are not exposed in the Past recap.
- **D-27:** Audio follows the existing retention policy. After a clip expires or is purged, the Past recap remains available with transcript and feedback and shows **Recording expired** in place of playback.
- **D-28:** Past recaps are view-only: students cannot edit, resume, re-record, or resubmit a completed mission from history.

### the agent's Discretion
- Choose the minimal additive schema for per-teacher/per-assignment review receipts and per-class review policy while preserving server-owned status transitions and auditability.
- Choose the polling/revalidation mechanism that satisfies the approximately-30-second freshness contract without requiring a general notification-event platform.
- Choose exact responsive breakpoints, empty/loading/error states, accessible labels, focus handling, and overflow-menu mechanics while preserving the approved hierarchy and behaviors.
- Choose the pagination mechanism and URL/query-state shape, provided ordering is stable and the five-item Past-page contract holds.
- Reuse existing transcript, audio, and pronunciation evidence rather than duplicating stored content.

</decisions>

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `bucketAssignmentStudents()` already maps one assignment's rows into completed, not-started, missed, retry, and teacher-review groups; retain this assignment-scoped contract for drill-downs.
- `StatusBadge` and `AssignmentListItem` provide existing status vocabulary and behavior that can be adapted to the new table/list presentation.
- `getAttemptEvidenceForTeacher()` and the teacher evidence page already provide transcript-first attempt detail, optional audio, pronunciation diagnostics, and teacher override actions.
- `listStudentAssignments()` already enforces student scoping via the unlocked student ID, computes late status at read time, and filters canceled assignments.

### Established Patterns
- Teacher pages are authenticated, dynamically rendered, and constrained by RLS-backed ownership checks.
- Students have no Supabase Auth account; student history must remain scoped through the existing short-lived unlock/PIN session and server-only service client.
- Assignment status transitions are server-owned and audited. Review receipts are a teacher-workflow layer and must not silently overload or mutate student completion state.
- Teacher evidence stays transcript-first with audio and pronunciation as optional/additive detail.
- Late assignments remain launchable; due-date presentation must not close student work automatically.

### Integration Points
- Replace the current teacher landing content in `src/app/teacher/page.tsx` with the shared teacher workspace shell and cross-class queue queries.
- Rework the class page and assignment review page to share navigation, counts, filters, and highlighted-student drill-down behavior.
- Extend teacher evidence actions with Mark reviewed and review-receipt recovery without changing the underlying completed status.
- Split `StudentHomeShell` data into Current/Past collections and add stable pagination.
- Add a student-owned, read-only evidence loader and recap route that reuses existing attempt-turn transcripts, audio metadata, Coco lines when available, and pronunciation results.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The teacher workspace should feel like Gmail: one newest-first work queue, bold/blue unread rows, review counts, filters, and All activity as durable history.
- Do not reproduce the rejected compressed three-card comparison. The accepted previews used one properly scaled full-width screen with a slim sidebar and one primary job per view.
- Needs review and Incomplete are separate mental models: submitted work to process versus unsubmitted work to follow up.
- The student Past detail should read like a reconstructed conversation with Coco, not a raw evidence table. Use child-friendly labels such as **You said** and **View what I said**.
- The visual result should closely match the approved browser previews created during brainstorming, including the sidebar hierarchy, full-width rows, status pills, responsive collapse, Current/Past tabs, and conversation bubbles.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- Email digests, immediate email, browser push, SMS, and other external notifications — future phase only if teachers need notices without opening Coco English.
- General-purpose notification event/delivery infrastructure — unnecessary for the in-app-only freshness contract in this phase.
- Student access to earlier failed/retry attempts — the Past recap intentionally shows only the final completed attempt.
- App-wide visual unification across voice, mascot, and Coco Chat — remains Phase 12; Phase 10.1 applies the approved operational layout to the teacher workspace and student history surfaces only.

</deferred>
