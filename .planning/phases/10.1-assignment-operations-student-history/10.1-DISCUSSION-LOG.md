## Current versus Past Rules

| Question | Options considered | Selected |
|----------|--------------------|----------|
| What enters Past | Completed only; completed + missed; completed + missed + canceled | Completed only |
| Current ordering | Retry/due soon/later; due-date only; newest assigned | Retry, due soon, later/no due date |
| Past organization | Five per page; grouped by month; ten per page | Newest completed first, five per page |
| Retry history | Final only; final + collapsed earlier tries; all attempts | Final completed attempt only |

**User's choice:** Keep actionable late/missed work in Current, hide canceled work, and make Past a simple history of final completed attempts.
