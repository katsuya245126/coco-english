<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Browser IDs → service-role actions | BOLA/cross-teacher mutation | Ignore browser tenant identity; authenticate teacher and constrain nested ownership before writes |
| Queue snapshot → client poller | Sensitive evidence disclosure | Minimal metadata allowlist; no transcript/audio/notes |

## STRIDE Register

- **T-10.1-01 / Elevation / BLOCK HIGH:** ID-only service-role mutations can cross tenants. Remove the path and prove zero writes in cross-teacher tests.
- **T-10.1-02 / Tampering / BLOCK HIGH:** Reopen must not revert completed status. Restrict it to receipt.reviewed_at.
- **T-10.1-03 / Information Disclosure / MITIGATE:** Poll response exposes only student/assignment/class labels and owned href.
</threat_model>
