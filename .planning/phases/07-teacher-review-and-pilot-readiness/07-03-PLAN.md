<objective>
Deliver the teacher-acts / student-reopens vertical slice. On the attempt detail page the teacher sees the two missing REV-04 facts (attempt count, highest hint level) and gets a three-way override control (mark complete / send for retry / keep in review) behind a confirmation step. The override is a server-owned, audited status transition. When the teacher sends an attempt for retry, the homework genuinely reopens for the student — both student-flow gates that currently block re-entry are fixed.

After this slice a teacher can, end-to-end, review an attempt and change its outcome — and a "needs retry" decision results in the student actually being able to re-record, not just a label (D-10).

Purpose: Satisfies REV-04 (complete attempt-detail evidence), REV-02 (attempt count + highest hint surfaced in detail per D-07), REV-06 (manual override with confirmation + audit per D-08/D-09), and the D-10 needs-retry reopen path.
Output: Extended evidence query/type, the override server action, the OverrideControls UI, and the two student-flow gate fixes.
</objective>
