<objective>
Wire the persistent `MascotStage` into `MissionFlowShell` above the single step-card area (D-01) and bridge the amplitude/playing signal from whichever `CocoSpeechAudio` is currently mounted (inside the active `Step*` component) up to the shell-level mascot. Then verify — via manual real-device checkpoints — that the stage persists with no layout shift, the speaking state tracks the real audio clock, and the mission flow never blocks on mascot performance.

Purpose: This is the integration plan that makes MASCOT-01..04 true end-to-end. The shell owns stable amplitude/playing callback handlers (ref-backed) that `MascotStage` reads and that are threaded down through each `Step*` component into its `CocoSpeechAudio` (added in Plan 02). The mount is a single unconditional JSX insertion so the stage never unmounts as `flow.step` changes.

Output: `MissionFlowShell.tsx` (mount + callback plumbing) and the five `Step*` components (each forwarding two new optional props to its `CocoSpeechAudio`), plus the three manual human-verify checkpoints (mount/no-layout-shift, real-audio speaking, real low-end device).
</objective>
